  5. [04] Weld analysis (GOM)
    where/wo: Probe S1x-NN (rating) + S1x-NN-SCAN (files) > Section '04 Weld analysis'

    files/Dateien: Evaluation export
  6. [05] Series assignment (randomisation)

  13. [12] Fracture surface analysis
